# Account and Data Deletion — FarmRisk

**Last updated:** 4 August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your **FarmRisk** account and the associated data, as required by Google Play.

## How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly within the app:

1. Open the **FarmRisk** app.
2. Go to **Account Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Once confirmed, your account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted from our active systems.

If you are unable to access the app, you may also request deletion by emailing us at **vegadurmin@gmail.com** from the phone number or contact details associated with your account.

## What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove the following data:

- Your **name**
- Your **contact number** (used as your account identifier)
- Your **crop details**
- Your **farm field demarcation / boundary data**
- Your **saved and favorite locations**
- Your **account profile and login credentials**

## What Data May Be Retained

- **Anonymized or aggregated data** (such as usage statistics and diagnostic logs) that is no longer linked to your identity may be retained to help us maintain and improve the Service.
- We may retain limited information where required to comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations, for only as long as legally necessary. Any such data is securely stored and access-restricted.

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed immediately upon in-app confirmation. Requests made by email are actioned within a reasonable period after we verify your identity.
